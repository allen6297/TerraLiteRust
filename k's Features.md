# Players

## Gameplay
- Voxel shapes (like the clutter no more mod)
- Limited world sizes (small, medium, large, mega) [biomes scale at with different sizes to not limit acess to specific resources]
- different ores in different stone types (like hytale) [breaking the ore 'replaces' it with that stone type]

### Ease of use
- mods on the server are not required to be installed on the client(hytale style)
- built in emi [recipe book for craftables, recipe and use viewer etc]

### Weather/Time

- dynamic day night cycle with seasons.
- dynamic weather collides with seasons (rain, snow, windy, foggy, etc)
